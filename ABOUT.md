# About — AI Resume Builder & Analyzer

A full-stack **AI Resume Builder & Analyzer** built on the **MERN stack** (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) and powered by large language models (Gemini & OpenAI) to turn resume writing into a fast, intelligent, career-launching experience.

Deployed link : https://summer-internship-project-ai-resume.vercel.app/

It takes you from a blank page to a polished, ATS-optimized resume through a guided **step-by-step wizard** — Personal Info, Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications, and Languages — with a **real-time live preview** inside an interactive 3D diary interface. You can even **import an existing resume** (PDF, Word, or TXT) and let the AI auto-extract your details, auto-save them, and kick off an instant analysis.

Once your resume is ready, the platform runs a **comprehensive AI intelligence suite** that goes far beyond grammar:

- **ATS & Job Description Match** — score your resume against a real job description to see how well you'd pass applicant tracking systems.
- **Skill Gap Analysis** — spot missing or underdeveloped skills and get actionable recommendations to close them.
- **Keyword Density & Context** — measure industry keyword usage, frequency, and relevance.
- **Context Quality & Impact** — evaluate every bullet point for quantified results, action verbs, and rich context.
- **Tech Stack Recommendations** — get modern, role-relevant technologies to learn or highlight.
- **Interview Questions** — generate tailored technical, behavioral, and system-design questions from your resume.
- **History & Analytics Dashboard** — track your score progression across analyses to see your improvement over time.

The platform is **provider-agnostic** (swap Gemini or OpenAI with a single env variable), **secure** with rate limiting and Helmet, and ships with a **flexible MongoDB setup** that falls back to an in-memory database for instant local development. A sleek **client-side authentication portal** (Google, Apple, Email/Password, or SMS-style OTP) completes the experience.

> **From cloud-ready import to AI-optimized, career-ready output — build smarter, analyze deeper, and land the job.**
